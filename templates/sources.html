Sources & limitations

How this works: every day, this tracker searches Google News, GDELT's global news index, and a set of PR wire feeds for stories mentioning a monitored UN agency, INGO, or NGO alongside donation-related language, published within the last {{ stats.max_article_age_days }} days. Candidate stories are grouped into events, read by an AI model, and turned into the structured entries you see on the daily page. Anything the model is unsure about is flagged under "Assumptions & uncertainties" on that entry rather than silently resolved.

What this will miss: donations that never appear in general news or PR wires at all — small or local gifts, donations announced only on social media, or ones disclosed only in an annual report months later. This tracker is a discovery aid for otherwise-uncaptured private donations, not a comprehensive financial record.

Confidence scoring: each entry is scored out of 10 on three factors: the strongest source type behind it (an official press release scores highest, a general news mention lower), whether an explicit figure was stated, and whether more than one independent outlet reported it. Entries scoring below {{ low_confidence_threshold }} are still published but marked low-confidence and excluded from the weekly email highlights.
